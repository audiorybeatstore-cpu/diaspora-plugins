Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about licensing, billing, or compatibility? We have answers.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, absolutely! You can cancel your monthly or annual plan at any time through your user dashboard. Your plugins will remain fully functional until the end of your paid billing cycle.
How many computers can I activate my plugins on?
Monthly passes include 2 machine authorizations, Annual passes include 4, and Lifetime licenses include 10. You can manage or revoke active machines easily inside your Diaspora account dashboard.
Do I need an iLok USB dongle?
No iLok physical dongle is required! We use the **Diaspora Central Installer** for instant online machine authentication and automatic updates directly to your DAW.
What happens to my projects if my subscription expires?
Your DAW sessions will continue to load, and your plugin parameters/settings will remain saved. However, processing will be bypassed until your subscription is renewed or reactivated.
